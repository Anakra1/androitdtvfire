Crimson Blood Requiem · User guide

Your TV sources.
Easy to keep current.

Connect a PC or phone, renew Sports Streams and refresh your channels without typing long links with the TV remote. These instructions match CBR 0.12.0.

Know which page you need

Three places, one setup.

  • This public website provides instructions and CBR downloads.
  • CBR’s paired setup page runs on your Kodi device. Open the address shown on your TV to edit that device’s sources. This public website does not connect to the TV or store its pairing code.
  • The provider’s website generates a configured manifest, or publishes an M3U channel list. Copy that link into CBR’s paired setup page.

CBR → Live TV browses provider channels and sports. Kodi’s main TV section uses IPTV Simple Client and the sources selected for its native guide. Adding a provider and selecting its channels for Kodi TV are separate steps.

Step 1

Connect a PC or phone.

  1. On your TV: open Kodi → Add-ons → Video add-ons → Crimson Blood Requiem → Source setup (phone / PC). It is also available inside CBR Live TV. Leave the screen showing the address and six-digit code open.
  2. Join the same home network. Your PC or phone and the Kodi device must be on the same local network. Ethernet and Wi-Fi can work together on the same router. Guest Wi-Fi may keep devices separate.
  3. Open the exact address on your TV in the browser’s top address bar. Replace the entire current address. Include http://, every dot and the port number after the second colon. Press Go or Enter.
  4. On the page headed Connect to your TV, enter the six-digit pairing code and press Connect. Your source list and editing tools appear.
  5. Keep Kodi’s setup screen open while you work. The session lasts up to ten minutes. Press OK on the TV when finished. If it expires, open Source setup again and pair with the new code.

Example only: http://192.168.1.50:8768/. Use your TV’s displayed address; its numbers or port may differ. Use http:// with the colon, not https:// or http//.

Use two tabs on your phone.

Keep the paired CBR page in one tab. Use the browser’s tab button or + to open the provider website in another. Copy its generated link, switch back to CBR, tap the URL field and paste. You do not need two browsers or two windows.

Use two tabs on your PC.

Open the provider in a second tab, copy its link with Ctrl+C, return to CBR and paste with Ctrl+V. You are editing the Kodi device whose address you opened, even if it is a Fire TV in another room.

Step 2 · Renew a personal provider link

Update Sports Streams.

Open Sports Streams settings ↗

  1. Open the settings page above on your phone or PC. Complete its human-verification step yourself if requested.
  2. Choose sports and leagues. If you want its live channel catalogue, check the Catalogs → 24/7 channels options. Save your settings where offered.
  3. Go to the provider’s Install section and choose Copy link, sometimes labelled Copy Manifest URL. Copy the complete generated addon link. You do not need to install Stremio to copy a link for CBR.
  4. Return to the paired CBR tab. Under Your sources, find Sports Streams and press Edit. Replace Manifest or M3U playlist URL with the copied link.
  5. Choose Live TV category → Live Sports, leave Enabled in CBR checked and press Save changes. Leave Programme country on Automatic for mixed-country sports.
  6. Press Refresh catalogues. Reopen the sports category in CBR Live TV or use Refresh listings. If those channels are also used in Kodi’s main TV guide, follow the TV refresh steps below.

Use the full configured link, not just the ID. The generated URL carries your configuration/ID. The bare https://sports.highfly.dev/manifest.json is a public default and may return renewal notices instead of your configured streams. A /configure webpage is not a manifest.

CBR accepts complete https:// and stremio:// addon links. Keep personal IDs and configured URLs private. Sports Streams and StreamsPPV are separate providers: edit the entry belonging to the link you generated.

Editing any source

Save it in the right place.

  1. On the paired page, press Edit beside an existing source to replace its old URL without leaving an expired duplicate.
  2. Paste the full link into Manifest or M3U playlist URL and choose its category from the directory above.
  3. Use Programme country United Kingdom for a UK-only source, or Automatic for mixed-country sources. This helps match schedules; it does not translate audio or change the stream’s country.
  4. Press Save changes and wait for the save result. For a genuinely new provider, use Add a source and Add source.
  5. Press Refresh catalogues, then reopen the listing in CBR. Also refresh selected sources for Kodi TV below if you use the native guide.

Use a provider manifest or M3U channel list, not a website page or a single channel’s video/HLS link.

Step 3 · Kodi’s native TV section

Refresh channels and choose groups.

  1. On the paired page, scroll to Kodi TV and guide. Select the channel catalogues you want and press Save TV sources.
  2. Press Build / refresh TV. Keep Kodi open while the build runs. Use Reload TV status / channel list to see the result.
  3. For a first-time TV setup, return to the TV and open CBR → Live TV → Kodi TV and guide → Set up Kodi TV / IPTV Simple Client. Kodi needs its compatible official PVR IPTV Simple Client.
  4. In CBR 0.12.0 and later, you can run CBR → CBR help, repair, updates and recovery → Check and repair TV (channels and guide). Stop playback first. The repair names each source, shows elapsed time and gives a summary.
  5. Open Kodi’s main TV screen. With the CBR skin, this opens the guide. Use Left/Right on the top tabs for UK TV, Pluto TV, Movies, Sports and other available groups; press Down to browse. Movies here means live movie channels.

A public M3U URL normally stays the same: refresh it rather than generating a personal manifest. A refresh cannot create missing channels or repair an upstream outage. Use Channel status / find a missing channel and Check channel links to distinguish missing entries from failing links. A sample response does not prove uninterrupted playback.

Organise your channels.

On the paired page, use Find a channel. Edit names, numbers, favourites, visibility and groups, then press Save channel changes. Hidden channels remain in CBR’s settings. Source checks do not automatically hide or remove channels.

Schedules are separate from streams

Update programme information.

CBR includes public schedule feeds for its supported UK, Samsung and Pluto sources. On the paired page, find Programme information and press Update programmes. Rebuild or repair Kodi TV afterwards if its native guide has not refreshed.

For another feed, use Add a programme feed. Enter Feed name, XMLTV URL (.xml or .xml.gz) and Country, then Save programme feed. Put XMLTV links here, not in the manifest/M3U field.

Where no real listing matches, the guide says Programme information unavailable - watch live. This notice does not block playback and is not a scheduled programme. Guide data and audio language are separate.

Three kinds of update

Choose the right update.

  • CBR software: use Check CBR component updates in the tools menu, or the Android / Fire TV and Windows update instructions. Kodi controls repository updates.
  • Personal provider link: copy a current configured link from that provider’s own site and save it in the paired page. Updating CBR software does not renew Sports Streams verification.
  • Lists and schedules: use Refresh catalogues, Build / refresh TV and Update programmes to refresh the existing sources.

Quick answers

If something does not connect.

The phone searches Google for the IP address.

Use the browser’s top address bar, replace all its text and type the TV’s exact address starting with http://. Include the colon after http and the port. Do not use a search box on the Google page.

The local page cannot be reached.

Leave Source setup open in Kodi, use the current displayed address and check both devices share the same local network. Mobile data, guest Wi-Fi, a VPN blocking local access or a Windows firewall can prevent connection. For Windows Kodi, allow Kodi on your private home network if needed. Do not open router ports. Reopen Source setup if the session expired.

I cannot use CBR and the provider site together on my phone.

Use two tabs: CBR in one, the provider in the other. Copy the generated link, switch tabs and paste it into CBR. If the ten-minute session expires, reopen Source setup and pair again.

Sports still says update, reinstall or enter a new manifest.

Check the correct Sports Streams entry was edited and saved with its full configured link, not only an ID, the bare manifest or the configure webpage. Complete any provider verification yourself, copy the current Install link again, then refresh catalogues and selected Kodi TV sources. A different provider such as StreamsPPV needs its own link.

A channel is in CBR Live TV but missing from Kodi TV.

Select its catalogue under Kodi TV and guide, Save TV sources and Build / refresh TV. Check its visibility and group. The native TV list contains selected live catalogues, not movie/series catalogues.

I moved the Fire TV or paired a different Kodi device.

Open Source setup on the device you want to edit and use its current local address and code. Provider links may need verification or renewal on another network, depending on that provider. Keep personal backups private.

Provider links checked 17 November 2026. Third-party settings pages, lineups and availability can change.

CBR 0.12.0 · Command interface

Titles, progress and sports in one place.

Update the video add-on, skin and wizard, then restart Kodi. The Android full installer is fulla040.zip; Windows uses full.zip. Run the wizard’s Full install / repair and confirm the skin change. A video-only update does not install the new skin. You can also select Settings → Interface → Skin → Crimson Blood Requiem.

  • Movies and Series: select a poster for its description, artwork, Play or Seasons and episodes, and watchlist toggle.
  • Continue watching: appears after confirmed playback reaches 30 seconds. Resume, start from the beginning, or play the next released episode when available. Progress stays on that device.
  • Sports: uses your configured Sports Streams artwork. Long-press a card to save it. Use Sports favourites, Teams & leagues and Upcoming; Search other sources looks for alternatives.
  • Setup: connect a phone/PC, edit sources, refresh catalogues and TV guide, repair TV or check updates. Advanced CBR tools and Kodi settings remain available.
  • Loading: Cancel exits source loading. Playback Cancel stops only the matching CBR video. A responding source can still fail later.

Sports needs your own working configured manifest. Updates preserve existing manifests; they cannot renew a provider’s human verification. With another Kodi skin, CBR uses native title menus.
