Sources without remote typing

Pair a phone or PC.

  1. In CBR, open Source setup (phone / PC). Keep the Kodi pairing screen open.
  2. Connect your phone or PC to the same home network as the Kodi device.
  3. Enter the exact http:// address shown by Kodi into your browser’s address bar, including its port number. Enter the six-digit pairing code.
  4. Choose Edit beside a source, paste its full configured manifest URL, and save. You can also add other providers.

The pairing page runs on your Kodi device and is available for up to ten minutes. It is separate from this public website.

Kodi’s native TV section

Build your channel guide.

  1. Open CBR → Live TV → Kodi TV and guide → Set up Kodi TV / IPTV Simple Client.
  2. Allow Kodi to install its official PVR IPTV Simple Client if needed. Let the channel build finish.
  3. Open Kodi’s main TV → Channels or TV → Guide, then choose the CBR TV, Freeview, Sky or other groups.
  4. In the paired PC page, use Kodi TV and guide to edit names, numbers and groups, hide channels or match missing programmes.

Group names and starter numbers are editable. They are not an exact regional provider lineup. Guide coverage depends on matching schedule data.

The optional crimson interface

Make the whole screen CBR.

  1. Install Crimson Blood Requiem Skin and CBR Setup Wizard from the CBR repository. The wizard is under Program add-ons.
  2. Open the wizard and choose Apply CBR space interface.
  3. To go back later, open the wizard and select Restore previous Kodi interface.

The video add-on works with Kodi’s normal skin too. Changing the skin keeps your existing provider settings in place.

Keep things current

Manifests and schedules.

CBR also accepts M3U channel lists. IPTV-org is included as an additional TV source, with the UK-specific playlist selected first and other countries selectable. Open CBR → Live TV → TV Channels → IPTV-org → United Kingdom. In the paired PC source page, other country choices are under Other M3U playlist countries. Channel availability and programme coverage vary.

A manifest tells CBR which catalogues or sources a provider offers. Some providers need a personal configured link, and that link may expire.

Use the complete link generated by the provider, including its configuration path or ID. A public default URL may not contain your channel choices.

Programme schedules are separate XMLTV feeds. The UK schedule feed is included; other countries may need their own feed added through the paired setup page.

Uninstall guides

Remove CBR in the right order.

Restore your previous skin, disconnect the CBR TV configuration, then uninstall the wizard, skin and video add-on. Each platform guide includes playback-engine cleanup and the choice to keep or remove saved settings.

Troubleshooting

A few common questions.

Kodi says a dependency failed to install. What now?

Check that you used the repository for your device. On Windows, install windowsrepo.zip, then choose CBR Windows Add-on Repository. Windows version 0.9.2 removes the unnecessary Android engine dependency.

On Android/Fire TV, use repository.cbr.zip and accept the CBR Playback Engine dependency. For a first install, use the repository route so Kodi can find dependencies.

“Could not retrieve directory information” appears in Kodi.

Check that the source is exactly https://anakra1.github.io/androitdtvfire/. Include https:// and check the spelling of androitdtvfire. Use the repository source address, not this website’s /website/ address or a GitHub project page.

Check the device’s internet connection, then reopen the source.

The phone or PC searches Google instead of opening the pairing page.

Use the browser’s address bar and enter the full address shown in Kodi, starting with http://. Keep the colon and port number. Do not change it to https://.

Both devices must be on the same home network. Keep the pairing screen open in Kodi. Guest Wi-Fi isolation or a VPN blocking local access can prevent the connection.

A sports or TV provider asks for a new manifest.

Generate a fresh configured manifest using that provider’s setup page. Open CBR’s paired phone/PC source manager, edit the existing provider, replace its full URL and save.

A provider’s plain default manifest may still return a setup notice. Keep personal configured links in your device settings rather than sharing them publicly.

A channel plays but has no programme information.

Playback and schedules come from different sources. In the paired setup page, filter channels by Missing programme listings. Expand Choose programme guide match, select the correct schedule and use Apply programme match.

Regional and +1 channels are kept distinct. Some channels have no available schedule. Restart Kodi to apply guide changes sooner than the normal PVR refresh.

One channel fails while others work.

The selected provider stream may be temporarily unavailable. Try again later, or check the channel in CBR’s backup Live TV browser, where the long-press menu includes a manual source chooser.

HTTP availability checks can skip blocked links, but cannot guarantee a stream decodes correctly or detect every picture loop.

The skin is still Kodi’s normal interface.

The video add-on and skin are separate installations. Install the CBR skin and setup wizard from your platform repository, then run the wizard and choose Apply CBR space interface.

Do my personal manifests move to a second device?

No. Each device keeps its own configured sources. Use its paired phone/PC setup page to add your links. Some providers may need a fresh configuration or verification for a different connection.

Release notes

The latest from CBR.

0.9.6

Faster TV channel selection

Recently responding links are rechecked and briefly reused in Kodi TV. CBR remembers the preferred provider/player for fresh resolution after the link expires, with availability checks and fallback to alternatives. These checks do not verify audio language. Restart Kodi after updating.

0.9.5

Fresh DaddyLive channel links

Added a website-backed TV source. CBR resolves supported player links when a channel starts, while the Kodi TV playlist keeps stable channel routes. Restart Kodi and open CBR once to add its UK lineup. Provider availability varies; paths requiring continuous proxy token renewal are excluded.

0.9.4

MediaFusion channel fallbacks

Enabled MediaFusion UK TV catalogues join the native TV lineup. Known channel-name aliases share alternative streams while preserving channel numbers and guide routes. Restart Kodi and open CBR once after updating.

0.9.3

M3U playlists, UK first

Added IPTV-org as an extra TV source using its UK-specific playlist, with other countries selectable. Import M3U lists through the source manager. Native TV selection checks other variants before choosing a link whose availability probe was inconclusive. Existing sources and channel customisations are retained.

0.9.2

Correct platform packages

Windows uses its bundled engine without the Android module dependency. Windows and Android package metadata now identify their platforms. Working Android 0.9.1 installations already include the channel and guide improvements.

0.9.1

A cleaner lineup, more programme matches

Spacing-only duplicates combine while preserving alternative links and existing numbers. Added known UK guide-name aliases, PC editor filters, pagination, bulk hide/show and manual guide matching.

0.9.0

CBR meets Kodi TV

Introduced local M3U and XMLTV output for PVR IPTV Simple Client, bringing supported channels and programme schedules into Kodi’s TV and Guide sections.
