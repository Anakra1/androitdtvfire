Connect to your TV

Keep the source setup screen open in Kodi. This connection lasts ten minutes.

Add or update live channel playlists and manifests. Choose which sources feed the Kodi TV guide below.

Live TV sources

Choose Edit to replace an expired manifest without entering it on the TV remote.

Add a source

For mixed-country sources, leave Automatic selected. Choose a country only when the source contains channels from that country.

Choose sources for the Kodi TV guide

Choose the channel sources for Kodi’s TV section. UK catalogues are selected initially. After the first build, run CBR → Live TV → Kodi TV and guide → Set up Kodi TV on the television.

Update the providers used for film catalogues and playback links. Sources supporting both films and series appear in both categories.

Update providers used for TV series and episode links. A shared provider has one saved configuration across Movies and TV Series.

Channel listings

Choose where each channel appears: UK TV, sports, internet TV or another guide group. Rename, number, hide or favourite channels here.

These are CBR’s custom channel numbers and groups. UK TV, internet channels and sports can be kept in separate groups. Channel numbers are preserved; Kodi may also apply its own channel sort order. The crimson CBR skin also styles Kodi’s native TV guide.

Programme information

Schedules are separate from streaming manifests. The UK feed is included automatically; other countries need a matching XMLTV feed. Programme times use the TV’s time zone.

Back up or move CBR to another device
Download your settings to this phone or PC, then upload the file through the paired page on the other device. Nothing is uploaded to a public website.
Without personal settings, the backup contains the TV channel layout only. Restoring keeps a local copy of the settings it replaces.
